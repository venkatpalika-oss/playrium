Your challenge

A solo adaptation of the Filipino line-tag game. Reach the far mint zone, then return to the starting zone. Defenders stay on their horizontal lines. Use arrows, WASD, hold the direction buttons, or drag on the court. Clear 3 courses; guards get faster. You have 3 lives across the run and 60 seconds per course. Each outward crossing scores 100; returning scores 200 plus remaining seconds.
