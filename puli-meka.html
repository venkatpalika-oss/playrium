Your challenge

Playrium board adaptation inspired by Aadu Puli Attam: 3 tigers, 15 goats, a 5×5 orthogonal board. This is not the traditional triangular board. Goats place all 15 before moving one step. Tigers move one step or jump one adjacent goat into an empty square. Tigers win at 5 captures; goats win by trapping every tiger. No legal move loses; 160 turns is a draw.
