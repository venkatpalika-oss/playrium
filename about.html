About Playrium

An interactive museum of the world — handcrafted, playful, and free to explore.

What is Playrium?

Playrium is a collection of small, handcrafted interactive experiences inspired by cultures around the world. Spin the globe, pick a country, and discover something playful — from gully cricket in India to goldfish scooping at a Japanese festival to sungka on a Philippine porch.

Every experience is built by hand to run right in your browser. No downloads, no accounts, no ads — just play.

How it works

Start on the home globe and click a glowing marker, or browse the complete catalogue. Feeling lucky? Try Surprise Me for a hidden pick, Relax & Satisfy for something calmer, or the daily challenge for a small goal to chase.

Your progress — recently played experiences and daily badges — is stored only on your own device.

Suggest an experience

Playrium keeps growing. If there's a game, toy, or tradition from your part of the world you'd love to see here, send a suggestion — we read everything.
