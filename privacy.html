Privacy

The short version: no accounts, no ads, and your progress never leaves your device.

What we collect

Playrium has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no forms. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information, and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

What stays on your device

To make the site feel personal, Playrium remembers a few things using your browser's local storage: which experiences you've recently played, your daily challenge badge, and small preferences like whether you've dismissed the globe hint. This data lives only in your browser, is never transmitted to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for playrium.fun.

Third-party services

Playrium loads a few resources from third-party content delivery networks: fonts from Google Fonts, the 3D globe library from unpkg, cdnjs, or jsDelivr, and country flag images from flagcdn.com. When your browser fetches these resources, those services receive your IP address as part of the normal working of the web, subject to their own privacy policies. Playrium does not share any other information with them.

Children

Playrium is a family-friendly site. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we also collect none from children.

Questions

If you have any questions about privacy on Playrium, email us at hello@playrium.fun.
