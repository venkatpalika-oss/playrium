Peteca Rally: rules

A solo arcade drill inspired by peteca hand play, not regulation competitive peteca. Move your hand under each descending shuttle. Returns happen automatically on contact. The flight accelerates; edge hits change its direction and score extra. Twenty returns wins; three drops ends the run.
